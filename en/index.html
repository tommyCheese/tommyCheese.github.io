Meta² teaches agents when to change their approach—and when to leave it alone

Meta² distills intervention guidance from contrasting training trajectories, then adapts an agent’s prompts, tools and memory rules within each test task. Pass rates reach 81.00% and 44.44% on two evaluations. Extra model calls are required; this is neither weight learning nor a guarantee of transfer across domains.

AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead. The report authors state that a companion paper was accepted at VLDB 2027.

Wolfpine makes Tensix computation and stalls observable

Wolfpine models Wormhole and Blackhole from public documentation and hardware measurements, adding optional timing estimates for compute, memory and communication. A single-tile Nekbone case has 3.9% total cycle error, but larger phase errors and 82% integer-division error limit generalization. SC26 confirms it as a RISC-V for HPC workshop research paper.
