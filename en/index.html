Train a multi-hop RAG retriever to recover from its own mistakes

A chain retriever’s wrong passages can distort later queries. Refreshing training states from the current retriever and mixing them with gold histories improves complete evidence coverage without changing its architecture or index. Long chains remain difficult, and search agents still lead on some metrics.

AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead. The report authors state that a companion paper was accepted at VLDB 2027.

Memory retrieval as continuous traversal: a small agent experiment and its limits
