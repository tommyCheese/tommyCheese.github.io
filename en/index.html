TeamGym separates communication failures from retrieval failures in agent teams

TeamGym compares one model with all pivotal evidence, a team given distributed evidence, and the same team searching source material. Across eight models and 12 source corpora, the main experiment reports mean success-rate losses of 22.9 percentage points from distribution and a further 19.9 points from retrieval, under controlled cooperative conditions.

AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead. The report authors state that a companion paper was accepted at VLDB 2027.
