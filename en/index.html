Results improve on two long-term memory benchmarks and controlled store comparisons, but reproducible execution does not remove extraction or compilation errors; implicit preferences remain difficult.

AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead. The report authors state that a companion paper was accepted at VLDB 2027.

PEEL extracts concept hierarchies from retrieval embeddings—with limits on exact edges

A small supervised probe over frozen embeddings decodes concept relations into taxonomy graphs. Two-domain tests with supplied concept vocabularies show structural transfer, but exact parent–child edges score much lower than graph structure, and downstream QA or retrieval gains are untested.
