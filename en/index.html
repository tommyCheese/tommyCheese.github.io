Can agents deliver an enterprise project? Requirements come before acceptance

An enterprise text-to-SQL study separates requirement discovery from delivery. Across 450 simulated conversations, agents recover about 60% of hidden facts; two of eight main delivery runs meet every gate. Production testing measures compilation validity, not SQL semantics, and one engagement plus automated judging limits generalization.

AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead. The report authors state that a companion paper was accepted at VLDB 2027.

GLAD filters relevant-looking evidence that can mis-train a fact checker

GLAD combines semantic coherence and lexical isolation to filter retrieved evidence. With contaminated training evidence but clean test retrieval, it recovers roughly 73–80% of the LIAR-RAW F1 loss. This is not proof of protection against online RAG injection. The paper appears in Findings of EMNLP 2026.
