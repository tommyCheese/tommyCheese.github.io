AMS balances sparse-matrix work and cache contention

AMS sizes work chunks by expected output and mixes tasks with different cache demands. Its two enhanced variants each beat their corresponding baseline on 19 of 20 displayed SuiteSparse matrices, while very sparse cases can lose to scheduling overhead.
