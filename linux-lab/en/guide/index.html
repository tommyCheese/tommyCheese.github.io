After learning, practice again

First go through the four-stage, 24-level introductory route, from sense of direction, document delivery to text screening, and then interspersed with different topics. Today's review is arranged according to the completion interval across days, mixing due topics and changing files and paths; after completing all ordinary levels of topics of the same level, the independent Boss actual combat will be unlocked. Combine commands in the same workspace to complete delivery, retention, troubleshooting, and recovery goals; Advanced and Master gradually add constraints. Depending on whether the final goal is achieved or not; update the mastery record after reviewing across days. Each topic also provides optional real-world terminal exercises.
